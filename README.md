# Christmas Village Planner — 3D

Interactive 3D plan for a ski-town Christmas village on a 73.5 × 16.5 in shelf in a wall niche. A cobblestone town plaza sits along the front with the carousel and popcorn diorama, the Candy Clock Tower and the Church & Skating Pond scene. A carved EPS mountain rises behind it, and the wooden houses and the Ski Chalet climb switchback ledges on both wings. There's a frozen mirror pond and crossed skis on the wall above the summit.

The whole scene is drawn in a storybook illustration style: cel shading, ink outlines, frosting snow with drips on the roofs and the foam ledges, a cream parchment backdrop and gentle falling snow (Night switches to a dusk sky). The carousel and popcorn diorama is rebuilt in that style on the footprint of a phone scan of the real piece. It has the upright popcorn cart, the turning carousel with bobbing horses, and globe lamp posts. Its card can show the raw scan (models/carousel-scan.glb, 1.2 MB). It's scaled to the measured 16.5 in length; its depth and height come from the scan's proportions, so they're estimates until measured.

Open: https://omarsimpson24.github.io/xmas-village-3d/

- Drag to rotate. Right-drag, shift-drag or two fingers pan, and scroll or pinch zooms. The on-screen pad pans, and ⟲ recenters.
- **Lights & motion** switches every light and every moving part on or off.
- **Move** lets you drag pieces onto the ledges (the layout is saved in this browser).
- **Plan** gives a top view with dimensions, **Layers** separates the foam layers, and **Cut list** lists every foam piece.
- **Wall** shows or hides the niche and skis. **Notes** lists which sizes are measured and which still need measuring.

- **Catalog** searches 1,575 Lemax and Make Market products by name, brand, category, lighted or animated. Tap **I have this → Add to model** to drop a stand-in at the product's published size into the first free flat spot. Then drag it, tap it for its product card, or remove it. **This is my…** links a piece that's already in the plan (H1–H8, LODGE, D1–D5…) to the real product, and its card and size update to match. **My inventory** lists everything with product links and a custom-piece form. Your pieces are saved in this browser.
- **Get illustrated** copies the pieces that still need a storybook illustration, with a share link (`?inv=`) that rebuilds the same inventory on another device. The ⚙︎ tab can send new pieces to an illustration webhook automatically. Its URL and key are saved only in your browser.
- **Library** has step-by-step guides (mountains, cobblestone, buildings, lights, snow, ponds, trains, motion, trees and figures, power, storage). Part cards link straight to the matching guide.

Catalog data comes from lemaxcollection.com (sizes are each product page's published "Approx. Size") and michaels.com search listings for Make Market. Lemax doesn't show prices; Make Market prices are Michaels list prices seen Nov 18, 2026. Product images belong to their makers and are linked to their product pages.

Prices are estimated ranges taken from store listings and can change.
